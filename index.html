About Me

The Vision

Public health interventions are only as powerful as the data systems that support them. My career is driven by a singular mission: to eliminate the architectural chasm between frontline clinical realities and high-level health information software. I believe that modernizing global health requires moving past rigid, parallel data collection methods and moving toward fluid, interoperable digital ecosystems built on robust Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability, and Learning (MERL) frameworks.

Health, Evaluation & Systemic Expertise

With an advanced foundation in public health and a decade of field-tested experience, I have designed and operationalized complex MERL architectures for multi-sector humanitarian and development programs. My background spans managing multi-site randomized controlled trials, establishing community accountability mechanisms, and steering rigorous data quality assessments (DQA/RDQA) alongside international NGOs and ministries of health. I specialize in translating intricate clinical requirements into highly structured indicator tracking metrics that ensure absolute regulatory and protocol compliance.

The Technical Edge

What sets my practice apart is the ability to step away from the evaluation framework and sit directly at the terminal to write production-ready code. As a full-stack developer, I build agile health-tech infrastructure using tools like React, Node.js, Python, and SQL to create responsive applications and customized dashboard environments. Whether optimizing enterprise setups like DHIS2 Tracker & Android Capture, deploying open-source EMR architectures like OpenMRS and Bahmni, or constructing customized data analysis pipelines in SPSS and STATA, I engineer data architectures that keep critical information clean, verified, and accessible.

The Intersection

Global health challenges demand a translator who speaks health policy, data science, and software engineering fluently. By standing firmly at the intersection of these fields, I bring a unique value proposition to global health organizations, ministries, and health-tech startups. I take projects seamlessly through their entire lifecycle—from diagnosing system gaps and writing custom full-stack code to evaluating national programmatic impact.
